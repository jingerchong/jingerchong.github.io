I learned how to use a mill and a lathe to make a flashlight in 2.670 Mechanical Engineering Tools.

This course was a 3-day class taken during IAP. We were briefed on operating several machines, with the end goal of producing a small flashlight. The head of the flashlight was mostly made on a mill, while the body was made on a lathe.
