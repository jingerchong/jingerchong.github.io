A structured multitask variational Gaussian process model for probabilistic full-body human motion prediction, using joint-dimension factorization and a continuous 6D rotation representation. Outperforms baselines including Motron (CVPR 2022) on the Human3.6M benchmark, with empirical coverage analysis and ablations over kernel choice, inducing points, and latent dimensionality.
