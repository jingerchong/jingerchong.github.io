Built a visual localization pipeline for an inspection crawler traversing 50-75 m wind turbine blades. Perspective views rendered from equirectangular imagery were chunked into overlapping windows and matched sequentially with ALIKED and LightGlue, then refined through COLMAP structure-from-motion with a cascaded coarse-to-fine geometric verification filter. The resulting single-node pipeline processes a blade in 2-5 hours and is slated for deployment.
