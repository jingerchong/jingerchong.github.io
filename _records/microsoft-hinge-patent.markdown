Owned the end-to-end design and prototyping of a multi-function hinge, from concept through alpha. Translated benchmarking and user-study findings into specs and first-order models for spring selection, material choice, and friction tuning, then converged on a target torque profile through iterative fabrication and measurement of 3D-printed and CNC-machined prototypes.
