Adapted H.265 video encoding to compress raw LiDAR data up to 50:1 for low-latency streaming from remotely piloted helicopters. Built a sensor-emulation pipeline from recorded video and packet captures to test perception pipelines without physical hardware, and reduced bandwidth up to 3x through azimuth-level decimation and adaptive bitrate control.
