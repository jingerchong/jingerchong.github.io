## Problem

Plan robust quadrotor–bottle maneuvers across changing payload conditions.

## Approach

Modeled quadrotor–bottle dynamics and formulated direct-collocation constraints for transitions, collisions, contact and impulse forces, and states.

## Results

Demonstrated robust bottle flipping across four fill levels from 25–100%.
