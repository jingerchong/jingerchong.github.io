## Problem

Model a pool-playing robot well enough to plan and execute shots in randomized simulation.

## Approach

Modeled cue dynamics, ball motion, and collisions, then combined a heuristic task planner with inverse kinematics and inverse-dynamics control.

## Results

Sank target balls consistently in randomized simulations.
