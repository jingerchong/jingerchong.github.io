During a research internship at National Chiao Tung University's Computer Graphics and Intelligence Lab, I trained a temporal-difference learning agent to play 2048. The agent learns a value function over board states and selects moves greedily with respect to that estimate, reaching a 93% win rate.

Full C++ implementation on [Github](https://github.com/jingerchong/).
