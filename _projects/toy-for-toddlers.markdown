Through MIT's [Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program](https://urop.mit.edu/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" }, I designed a research instrument for the [Early Childhood Cognition Lab](https://eccl.mit.edu/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" }: a box with interchangeable modules for studying how toddlers explore during free play. I designed and tested 3D-printed threaded connectors for attaching the modules, then assembled the main box from panels, attached the connectors, and painted the enclosure.
