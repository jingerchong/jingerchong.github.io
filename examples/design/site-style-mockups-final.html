Now

I'm currently drafting my PhD thesis proposal on human intent prediction for autonomous navigation, as part of the Center for Complex Engineering Systems (CCES), a collaboration between MIT and King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST) in Saudi Arabia.

Robots that move through spaces shared with people need to anticipate where those people are going, not just react to where they are. My work focuses on the conditions that make this hard in practice: predictions that run in real time on board the robot, environments that change as the robot moves through them, and uncertainty estimates that a planner can use to stay safe.

Underactuated Robotics · 2023

Water Bottle Flipping Quadrotor

Role
[your role]
Team
[team size]
Stack
DrakePython

Video·Report (PDF)

I posed flipping a water bottle with a quadrotor as a hybrid trajectory optimization and solved it with direct collocation in Drake. In simulation, the planner produced robust flips across four fill levels, from 25% to 100%.

The goal was to plan quadrotor–bottle maneuvers that stay robust as the payload changes. The amount of water in the bottle changes how it moves, so a flip tuned for one fill level may not work for another.

I posed the flip as a hybrid trajectory optimization with a fixed mode sequence and solved it with direct collocation in Drake. The constraints encode the quadrotor and water-bottle dynamics, the desired state at each mode transition, collision avoidance, contact forces, and impulse and collision dynamics.

Comparing fill levels

I then compared the resulting trajectories across water fill levels, and the optimizer found a robust flip at each of the four levels tested, from 25% to 100%.

What the pass removed or merged

  • Mono sizes: six (0.76 / 0.78 / 0.8 / 0.82 / 0.84 / 0.9em) became one, 0.8em. Labels and dates differ only by color and weight.
  • Weights: 300 / 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 became 300 (hero only), 400 and 700. 600 is kept only for mono labels and buttons, where Plex Mono needs it to read as bold.
  • Text colors: #111, #333 and #3a4248 became one text color (#111) plus grey (#646E78) for meta. Secondary lines use the Small size instead of a second gray.
  • Item titles: card titles (1.08em), paper titles (600 weight) and org names (1em bold) are now one Item title style.
  • Lists: the industry rows, Teaching lines and Service lines are now one .list component, with row padding tightened from 0.28em to 0.15em and line-height from 1.6 to 1.5, so it's about 20% denser.
  • Left-column grids: publications, featured rows and the About intro share one .lc grid and one --col width.
  • Entry header: Research and Education use the same "org bold / role italic + meta right" block.
  • Spacing: about 15 ad-hoc margins became four steps (0.4 / 0.9 / 1.6 / 3rem). They're in rem, so a section gap is the same size no matter which font size it sits next to.
  • Right edges: dates in lists and entries now end at the same right edge as the section rules and "All →" links.
  • Removed: industry cards, the timeline rail, the right-hand year on rows, and bold-and-highlighted metrics (now plain bold).
  • Kept on purpose: italic for roles and organization names in lists. It's the one sans variation, and it separates "what" from "where".
